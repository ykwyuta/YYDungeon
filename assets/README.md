# 画像素材

- YYMythology の `assets/mythology/wild-djinn.png` から氷の主と砂の魔神を切り出しました。
- YYMythology の `assets/units/` から作業員と冒険者3種を流用しました。作業員はゴブリン守備兵に合わせて着色しています。
- YYCrusaders の `assets/map/fort.png` を題字脇に使っています。
- ゴブリン親方・宝箱・針の罠・氷結の罠は組み込み ImageGen で新規生成しました。透過 PNG をトリミング・縮小し、罠のシートを2つに分けています。

## foreman

Use case: stylized-concept. Isolated 2D dark fantasy RTS sprite on genuine transparent background. Full body goblin construction foreman for a Japanese retro dungeon strategy game. Compact wiry moss-green goblin with large pointed ears and a grumpy kindly face, in a practical rough umber leather mason apron with a copper buckle, carrying a stone pickaxe and holding a rolled floor plan, three-quarter frontal view. Rich painterly medieval game sprite, crisp hand-painted material edges and strong readable silhouette at 64 pixels. Muted olive and brown, warm amber highlights and cool blue fill, match realistically painted medieval strategy unit art. Entire body and tools inside frame, no ground, no shadow outside silhouette, no text, no extra figures.

## chest

Use case: stylized-concept. One isolated closed medieval wooden treasure chest game sprite on genuine transparent background, for a top-down JRPG dungeon map. Broad domed lid, dark reddish wood, aged brass bands and clasp with small amber glint. Three-quarter elevated angle showing top and front, high contrast hand-painted realistic strategy-game style, chunky shape readable at 28 pixels. Centered with breathing room. No text, characters, floor, drop shadow or other objects.

## traps

Use case: stylized-concept. One small 2D dungeon trap spritesheet containing exactly two fully isolated objects in two side-by-side equal square cells on a genuinely transparent background, widely separated, never overlapping the central divider. LEFT: five short steel spikes protruding from a flat dark slate pressure plate, with worn bronze corners. RIGHT: a blue magical frost trap with a clustered faceted ice crystal rising from a dark stone rune base. Both shown from the same elevated three-quarter top-down JRPG angle. Rich hand-painted fantasy strategy-game sprites with clear silhouettes readable at 24 pixels, whole objects visible with generous margins, no ground, no text, no labels or extra objects.
